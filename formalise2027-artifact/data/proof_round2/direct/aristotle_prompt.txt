This Lean 4 project translates the HOL4 development of CakeML type soundness into Lean. The HOL4 sources are in hol4-ultramin/. Every theorem statement in LeanTypeSound/ was reviewed against its HOL4 source and is proved in HOL4, so treat each statement as correct and fixed.

Task: fill in the sorries of the theorems.

Rules:
1. Do not change any theorem statement. Do not weaken it, add hypotheses or type class assumptions, rename it, or delete it.
2. Do not delete, rename, or move declarations or files. Keep TypeSound.lean complete, including decs_type_sound and semantics_type_sound at its end.
3. You may add helper lemmas. You may also prove the termination obligations that are left as decreasing_by sorry. You may change the body of a definition only to prove its termination (for example with List.attach), and the new body must compute the same function.
4. Work in import order (see LeanTypeSound.lean): the prelude and definition files first, then NamespaceProps, Misc, SemanticPrimitivesProps, EvaluateProps, TypeSysProps, Weakening, and TypeSound last. Use lemmas that the project states earlier.
5. The HOL4 proofs in hol4-ultramin/ show the proof structure. Follow them when they help.
6. If you cannot prove a theorem, leave its sorry in place and continue with the next theorem.
7. At the end, the project must build with lake build without errors.
